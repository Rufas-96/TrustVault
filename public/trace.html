How a copy is stamped

  • txt
    Text & CSVA visible footer line names the reader and the trace ID.
  • pdf
    PDFA hidden comment after the end-of-file marker. The PDF opens normally.
  • docx
    Word, Excel, PowerPointWritten into the archive comment of the file.
  • img
    PNG & JPEGA metadata text chunk or comment segment.
Owners can only trace copies of documents they own. Administrators can trace everything. Every lookup is recorded in the audit trail.
Stamps can be removed by someone who knows to look, and a photo of the screen carries none. If the stamp is gone but the file is unchanged, its fingerprint still matches.
