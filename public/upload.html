What happens to your file

  • Its type is checked against its contents, not just its name.
  • It is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before it is written to disk.
  • It is stored under a random name with no public URL.
  • The upload is recorded in the audit trail with a SHA-256 fingerprint.
Web pages, SVG images, scripts and executables are refused, because they could run code in a reader's browser.
